Helpful Links

For Labrador lovers & new owners

A handful of resources we trust for breed information, health testing, and responsible ownership.

  • 🐕
    American Kennel Club — Labrador Retriever Official AKC breed standard, registration, and breed information.
  • 🏅
    The Labrador Retriever Club, Inc. (LRC) The AKC parent club for the Labrador Retriever breed.
  • 🩺
    Orthopedic Foundation for Animals (OFA) Health databases for hips, elbows, eyes, and genetic health testing.
  • 🔬
    Paw Print Genetics Canine genetic health and trait testing we use and recommend.
  • 👁️
    Canine Eye Registration Foundation (CERF/OFA Eyes) Eye examination registry for responsible breeding.
  • 🍖
    AKC Nutrition & Care Advice Expert guidance on feeding, training, and caring for your Lab.
