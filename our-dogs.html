Our Dogs

The champion English Labradors at the heart of Persimmon Hill.

CH Spirit Oaks Berghoff's Tropical Treat

AKC Champion Winners Dog Best of Winners

"Berghie" is our handsome conformation Champion — a classic English Labrador with a beautiful blocky head, correct structure, and the sweetest disposition. He finished his championship with notable wins, including Winners Dog and Best of Winners honors.

More than his titles, Berghie is a beloved member of our family. His gentle temperament and love of people shine through in his puppies.

View Berghie's Pedigree

GCH Spirit Oaks Isabella of Persimmon Hill

AKC Grand Champion Foundation Dam Beautiful Temperament

"Bella" is our stunning Grand Champion girl — the heart of the hillside. With her lovely feminine type, correct movement, and melt-your-heart expression, she represents everything we love about the English Labrador.

Bella is a devoted companion and a wonderful mother, passing on her beauty and her gentle nature to each of her puppies.

View Bella's Pedigree

Olivia of Persimmon Hill

OFA Excellent Fully Health Tested Outstanding Bloodlines

Olivia is our gorgeous black girl with a sweet, steady temperament. She has completed all OFA and genetic testing and boasts OFA 'Excellent' in every category — all documents are available upon request.

Behind that beautiful expression are outstanding bloodlines that we're proud to carry forward in our program.

View Olivia's Pedigree

Raven of Persimmon Hill

Beautiful Black Lab Expecting Mama Sweet Temperament

Raven is our lovely black girl — athletic, affectionate, and every bit the classic English Labrador in temperament and type. She's a joy around the farm and adored by everyone she meets.

We're thrilled that Raven is expecting her litter with Spirit Oaks "Blizzard" (see below), due late November 2026.

Sabre of Persimmon Hill

Cherished Companion Classic English Type Gentle Soul

Sabre is a handsome, light cream English Labrador with the broad head, substantial bone, and thick coat we treasure in the breed — here surveying the Blue Ridge from a mountain summit amid the autumn color.

Calm, devoted, and endlessly sweet, Sabre is a wonderful ambassador for everything Persimmon Hill stands for.
